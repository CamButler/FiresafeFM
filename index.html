Specialist Cleaning for Working Commercial Sites

Fire Safe FM supports UK businesses with professional kitchen extraction cleaning, ventilation hygiene and detailed commercial cleaning. From busy food premises to managed facilities, we focus on the areas that need specialist attention and a practical plan of work.

We hold a TR19 certificate and can discuss our relevant credentials when you contact us. Our kitchen extraction cleaning service is presented against the BESA TR19 Grease specification, while general ventilation hygiene is described separately under BESA TR19 Air.

Every site is different. We arrange an on-site inspection to understand access, system layout and the cleaning required before providing a tailored quotation.

Commercial Sectors We Support

We work with enquiries from restaurants, pubs, hotels, leisure facilities, educational sites, healthcare settings and facilities management teams. Tell us about the premises and the cleaning challenge; we will discuss the most suitable next step.

Hospitality and food service

Restaurants, pubs, hotels and catering operations rely on kitchens and facilities that can be cleaned and maintained around busy periods. We offer BESA TR19 Grease kitchen extraction cleaning and commercial deep cleaning enquiries for these environments.

Education, care and healthcare settings

Sites with high occupancy and sensitive operational requirements need careful scheduling and clear communication. We discuss the specific cleaning areas, access and site controls before any quotation.

Leisure and community facilities

Changing rooms, clubhouses and shared-use areas can require targeted deep cleaning and planned maintenance. We assess the space and agree the scope following a visit.

Facilities managers and multi-site operators

For businesses with a portfolio of locations, Fire Safe FM can discuss kitchen, ventilation and facilities cleaning requirements and the inspections needed to build a suitable proposal.
