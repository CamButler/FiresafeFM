Our story

Founded on getting the detail right

Fire Safe FM has been delivering high-quality commercial cleaning services since 2010. What started as a focused kitchen extraction service has grown into a full commercial cleaning operation covering ventilation systems, leisure facilities and commercial properties across the UK.

Our reputation is built on professionalism, competitive pricing and meticulous attention to detail, ensuring every facility we work on is left spotless, compliant and operating to the highest standards.

Because TR19 (DW/172) compliance sits at the centre of what we do, our teams are trained specifically for extraction and ductwork hygiene — not general cleaning stretched to cover it. That specialism is why the same clients keep bringing us back.
