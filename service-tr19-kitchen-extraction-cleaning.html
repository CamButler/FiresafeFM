TR19 Kitchen Extraction Cleaning

TR19 (DW/172) compliant extraction system cleaning with documented reporting.

TR19 (DW/172) sets the UK standard for cleaning kitchen extraction systems. We clean canopies, filters, ductwork and fans to this standard and provide reporting you can keep on file for insurance and compliance purposes.

  • Cleaning carried out to TR19 (DW/172) guidance
  • Covers canopy, ductwork, fan and filters
  • Post-clean verification and photographic reporting
  • Helps meet insurance and fire safety requirements
  • Scheduled visits to keep compliance current
