Commercial Kitchen, Extraction & Facilities Cleaning Services

Every commercial site has different equipment, usage levels and access considerations. Fire Safe FM provides specialist cleaning services for kitchens, ventilation systems and busy facilities. Our team can help identify the appropriate service after discussing and inspecting your premises.

BESA TR19 Grease Kitchen Extraction Cleaning

Specialist cleaning of commercial kitchen extract systems, assessed and scoped at inspection.

BESA TR19 Air Ventilation Cleaning

General ventilation and duct hygiene cleaning aligned to BESA TR19 Air.

Canopy & Grease Filter Cleaning

Cleaning where kitchen vapours first enter the extract system: canopies and grease filters.

Extractor Fan Cleaning

Specialist cleaning of commercial kitchen extractor fans and associated components.

Commercial Kitchen Deep Cleaning

Detailed commercial kitchen deep cleaning for restaurants, hotels and catering premises.

Facilities & Communal Area Deep Cleaning

Commercial facility deep cleaning for shared spaces, leisure sites and managed premises.

Post-Refurbishment & Post-Renovation Cleaning

Commercial post-refurbishment cleaning to prepare workplaces and facilities for use.

Planned Maintenance Cleaning

Planned commercial kitchen, ventilation and facilities cleaning for UK businesses.
