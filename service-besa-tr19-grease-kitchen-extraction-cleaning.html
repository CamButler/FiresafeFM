BESA TR19 Grease Kitchen Extraction Cleaning

Specialist cleaning of commercial kitchen extract systems, assessed and scoped at inspection.

Why extract-system cleaning matters

Cooking vapours carry grease particles into the canopy and extract route. Over time, residue can collect within ductwork, around fans and at points that everyday kitchen cleaning cannot reach. A planned specialist clean helps operators manage that build-up and the associated fire risk.

BESA TR19 Grease is the industry specification specifically concerned with grease accumulation in commercial kitchen extraction systems. It is distinct from general ventilation hygiene cleaning under BESA TR19 Air.

What the service can include

Following inspection, the agreed scope may cover canopy interiors, accessible plenum areas, filters, grease-laden extract ductwork and associated fan components. The extent of cleaning depends on the system design, condition, access arrangements and the findings at the site.

We discuss any obvious access or scope limitations before the quotation is prepared, helping you understand what has been inspected and what cleaning is proposed.

TR19 certificate and supporting information

Fire Safe FM holds a TR19 certificate. Ask our team for details of the certificate and the relevant supporting documentation when you arrange your inspection. Specific project reporting and any certificate issued for completed work should be confirmed as part of the agreed service.

Who this service is for

This service is relevant to restaurants, pubs, commercial caterers, hotels, schools, care facilities and managed kitchens where grease-laden extraction systems require specialist attention.
